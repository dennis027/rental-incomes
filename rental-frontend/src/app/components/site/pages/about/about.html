About us

Taking the paperwork out of renting

{{ site.name }} helps landlords and property managers run their buildings from one place: who lives where, what they owe, what they've paid and what the property is earning.

Why we built it

Most rental businesses still run on receipt books, spreadsheets and M-Pesa messages scrolled through at the end of the month. Arrears get missed, deposits get disputed, and nobody is quite sure what each building really earns.

{{ site.name }} replaces that with a single record for every property, unit and tenant. Invoices are generated for the whole building at once, paybill payments match themselves to the right tenant, and every report is built from the same numbers, so the dashboard, the statement and the receipt always agree.

Who it's for

  • Landlords with one building or many
  • Property managers and letting agencies
  • Caretakers and accountants who handle collections
