This policy explains what personal data {{ site.name }} (“we”, “us”) handles, why, and the choices you have. We process personal data in line with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.

Who is responsible for the data

Landlords and property managers who use {{ site.name }} (“customers”) decide what information about their tenants is entered into the system. For that tenant information the customer is the data controller and we act as their data processor: we store and process it only to provide the service to them. For information about our customers themselves, and about visitors to this website, we are the data controller.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, username, email address and password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Tenant and property records entered by customers: tenant names, phone numbers, ID numbers and ID photos, contracts, invoices, payments, meter readings, expenses and maintenance requests.
  • M-Pesa payment details received from Safaricom when tenants pay to a customer's paybill: transaction code, amount, time, account reference, phone number and payer name.
  • Messages you send us through the contact or demo forms: name, email, phone and your message.
  • Technical data: IP address and browser details in server logs, kept to keep the service secure and working.

How we use it

  • To provide the service: billing, receipts, statements, arrears tracking and reports for our customers.
  • To keep accounts secure, for example by limiting repeated failed sign-in attempts.
  • To reply to your messages and demo requests.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use tenant data for advertising.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run the system: cloud hosting for the application and database, an email provider to send account emails, and Safaricom for M-Pesa payment notifications. They may only use the data to provide their service to us. Some of these providers store data outside Kenya; where they do, we rely on the safeguards required by the Data Protection Act. We disclose data to authorities only where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Customer records are kept while the customer's account is active. Financial records such as invoices and receipts may need to be kept for the periods required by Kenyan tax law. Messages sent through our forms are kept only as long as needed to deal with them. When an account is closed, its data is deleted on request, subject to those legal requirements.

How we protect it

Data is sent over encrypted connections, access requires signing in, and uploaded ID documents are never publicly accessible. Our Security page describes these measures in more detail.

Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act you have the right to:

  • be told how your personal data is used;
  • access the personal data held about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected and false or misleading data deleted;
  • object to the processing of your data.

If you are a tenant, please contact your landlord or property manager first, since they control your records; we will help them respond. Anyone can contact us about their data. You also have the right to complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The dashboard stores your sign-in session and preferences, such as light or dark mode, in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in and the app remembers your choices. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page, and tell customers about significant changes before they take effect.
